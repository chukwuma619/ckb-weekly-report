## Summary

**Bootstrapped the monorepo** with `@fiber-peer-pay/node` (order queue, agent contract, merchant auth) and `@fiber-peer-pay/react` (optional buyer checkout UI). Defined the **Fiber Desktop Agents** integration model — sellers poll your API from Desktop; your app never calls FNN directly.
